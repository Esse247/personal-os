# Roadmap

## Foundation v0.1 — complete

Continuity system, architecture/security/domain contracts, mock-only vertical demonstration, typed API, responsive dashboard, persistence/migrations, synthetic fixtures, and independent evidence-backed verification.

## Foundation v0.2 — next: hardening

First establish PostgreSQL-first CI for migrations, fixtures, persistence, and concurrency. Then address production-grade identity/authorization design, explicit consent-grant lifecycle, transactional outbox/worker reliability, generated API client, browser automation, accessibility audit, retention/deletion design, deprecation removal, and expanded schedule property tests.

## Pilot v0.3 — consented read-only integrations

One provider at a time through sandbox/read-only scopes, a secret broker, sync checkpoints, revocation and deletion, provider contract evidence, operational monitoring, and explicit pilot consent. No payment or irreversible action.

## Later product increments

Chief of Staff routing and specialist evaluation; notifications and voice; household collaboration with mature consent; house-project workflows; finance planning; wearable/health observations; decision learning; and carefully authorized external actions. Each capability graduates independently from planned to mocked to implemented to live.

The roadmap is directional, not a promise of live capability.
